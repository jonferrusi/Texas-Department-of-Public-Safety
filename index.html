Our Mission

Protecting the Roleplay Community
of Liberty County

The Texas Department of Public Safety is a professional law enforcement roleplay division operating within Emergency Response: Liberty County. Our mission is to deliver realistic, respectful, and immersive law enforcement roleplay — protecting the citizens of Liberty County, upholding the law with integrity, and setting the standard for professionalism both on and off duty. Every Trooper who wears this badge is held to the same three principles the real seal has carried for generations.

I.

Courtesy

Every citizen and every call is met with respect, patience, and professionalism — no exceptions.

II.

Service

Our Troopers respond swiftly and reliably to serve Liberty County, on duty and in the community.

III.

Protection

We stand between Liberty County and harm — upholding the law fairly, firmly, and consistently.
